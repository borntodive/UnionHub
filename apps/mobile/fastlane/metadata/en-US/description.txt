UnionHub is the official app for FIT-CISL aviation union members. Manage your union membership directly from your phone.

KEY FEATURES:
• Payslip Calculator - Detailed breakdown of your salary with Italian tax calculations
• Issue Reporting - Report and track work-related issues directly to union representatives
• Documents - Access union documents, contracts, and communications
• Notifications - Real-time updates on union activities and important announcements
• Member Directory - Connect with other union members
• Biometric Login - Secure Face ID and fingerprint authentication
• Offline Support - Access key features even without internet connection

Designed for pilots and cabin crew, UnionHub puts your union membership in your pocket.

Stay connected. Stay informed. Stay protected.
